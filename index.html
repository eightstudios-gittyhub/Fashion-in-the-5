Event Details

What to Expect

Every detail of Fashion in the 5 is designed to give creatives and brands a premier platform to be seen.

01

Showcase Slots

5 – 15 Min

Each participating artist or brand is allocated a dedicated showcase window to present, perform, or display their work to a live audience.

02

Artist / Brand Entry

$150 – $200

Entry investment includes your showcase slot, designated space, and two complimentary guest passes for the event.

03

Additional Guests

$25 / Guest

Beyond your two included passes, additional guests may be added at the door at a flat rate per person.

04

Limited Spots

10 – 20 Brands

To preserve the curated nature of the event, showcase participation is strictly limited. Secure your spot early to avoid being placed on the waitlist.

05

Includes

2 Guests Free

Every entry includes two complimentary guest passes. Bring your team, your photographer, or your most loyal supporters.

06

Purpose

Build. Network. Grow.

Fashion in the 5 exists to cultivate a thriving local fashion and creative economy — where independent brands can connect, collaborate, and elevate one another.

Fashion in the 5 is not just an event — it is a movement for the independent artists, brands, and creatives of the Capital Region who are ready to be seen on their own terms.

Who Can Apply

Fashion in the 5 is built for the full spectrum of creative talent in the 518.

Fashion Designers

Present original collections, debut new pieces, and introduce your aesthetic to a curated local audience.

Clothing Brands

Set up your display, sell directly to consumers, and position your brand in front of fashion-forward shoppers and buyers.

Models

Walk the showcase, collaborate with brands in attendance, and grow your portfolio within the local creative scene.

Photographers & Videographers

Cover the event, shoot for brands, build your portfolio, and connect with artists who need your eye and your lens.

Artists & Performers

Whether you paint, sketch, or perform — if your craft intersects with fashion and creative culture, there is a place for you here.

Vendors & Product Sellers

Bring your product, your table, and your brand story. Fashion in the 5 gives you direct access to a quality consumer base.

Sponsors & Collaborators

Align your brand with a growing local fashion movement. Sponsorship opportunities are available for businesses, agencies, and organizations.
